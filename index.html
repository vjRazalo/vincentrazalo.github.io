About

Business, technology, people and delivery

Most technology problems start out as business problems. I work on both halves.

For more than ten years my work has moved between technology and the people who depend on it. I started out teaching software development, databases and systems analysis, went on to lead a college of computer studies as dean, and since 2020 have worked as an IT Specialist for FOCUSED.com, coordinating website implementations, solving technical problems and keeping systems documented and running.

That mix taught me where projects usually go wrong. Requirements are unclear, processes have grown up around workarounds, and the people who know the business and the people who build the systems don't always share a language. My job is to close that gap: understand what the organisation actually needs, shape it into something a team can build, and see it through to delivery.

In 2026 I completed a Master of Management Information Systems at Edith Cowan University in Perth. It added business strategy and information systems management to a technical foundation built on a Master of Science and a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology.

Australian Aviation Register Analytics & Visualisation Platform

Client
Business News
Role
Project Manager & Technical Lead
Team & timeframe
Team of five · Aug – Nov 2026
Source Aviation register

100,000+ records held across multiple database environments.

Process Integrate & clean

Python-based processing to bring the records into one consistent structure.

Match Entity resolution

Aircraft records linked to Business News company records, classified by confidence.

Use Search & visualise

Interactive search, filtering and charts for research and trend analysis.

Problem

Researchers were answering aviation questions by hand. Register records didn't line up with company records, so every enquiry meant manual searching, cross-checking and rebuilding the same reports.

Approach

A hybrid method: Waterfall for scope, requirements and milestones the client signed off, and Agile iterations to build and test the data processing and interface.

Leadership

Led a team of five as Project Manager and Technical Lead, and owned client engagement, requirements, quality assurance, testing and delivery.

Outcome

A central platform where over 100,000 integrated records can be searched, filtered and visualised, with aircraft linked to the companies behind them.

Business value

Manual research and reporting replaced by one shared tool, cutting the time each enquiry takes and giving the team data it can base decisions on.
